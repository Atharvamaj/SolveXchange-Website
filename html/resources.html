Rubric

SOLVEXCHANGE PITCH CONTEST RUBRIC

Lesson 1

The Importance of a Problem

This lesson shows that strong ideas begin with identifying meaningful problems and generating many possible solutions. Using creativity tools helps turn everyday challenges into impactful ideas.

Lesson 2

Problem Deep Dive & Community Validation

This lesson focuses on deeply understanding a problem before developing solutions. Key concepts include distinguishing between symptoms and root causes, identifying affected groups, validating problems through research and surveys, and forming clear problem statements.

Lesson 3

DESIGNING A HIGH-QUALITY, INNOVATIVE SOLUTION

This lesson shows how to turn an idea into a clear, well-structured solution that directly addresses a problem. It emphasizes defining how the solution works, why it stands out, and the real impact it will create over time.

Lesson 4

Implementation, Feasibility & Resources

This lesson shows that great ideas only matter if they can actually be executed. By building clear timelines, identifying resources, and stress-testing feasibility, students turn concepts into realistic, actionable plans with real community impact.

Lesson 5

Building a Winning Pitch

This lesson shows that even the strongest ideas need clear and compelling communication. By structuring their pitch, refining delivery, and making it memorable, students learn how to present their solutions in a way that captures attention and convinces judges.

Lesson 6

CONFIDENCE, PROFESSIONALISM & Q&A MASTERY

This lesson teaches students how to present their ideas clearly and handle live questions with confidence. By practicing webcam presence, vocal timing, and a structured response framework, students learn to answer judge feedback effectively and communicate the real value of their project.

Lesson 7

The Championship Mindset

This lesson shows that great ideas only matter if they can actually be executed. By building clear timelines, identifying resources, and stress-testing feasibility, students turn concepts into realistic, actionable plans with real community impact.

Exemplar

Taskit

This exemplar demonstrates how a student team builds a complete, data-backed community solution. By focusing on platform safety, guaranteed completion, and an itemized budget, TaskIt serves as a practical model for structuring a high-scoring pitch.
