Eligibility

  • Must be a student attending a high school in Canada
  • Within the grades of 9-12
  • Can be completed individually or in small groups (1-4 people)

    • Rubric Criteria

    You will be graded based off:
  • Feasibility/Viability
  • Creativity/Innovative
  • Professionalism
  • Value of Proposition (benefits and impact)
  • Sustainability
  • Clarity
  • Visual Appeal
  • Implementation Plan

    • How to enter our pitch contest for a chance to win prize money

    1.  Sign up to confirm your commitment to participating.

    2.  Attend our virtual, monthly seminars through the google classroom to receive guidance on creating your pitch, proposing a solution to a problem in your community. After each session, you will have to submit a monthly progress report to our Director of Training.

    3  Refine your pitch.

    4.  Submit an overview of your pitch to our Director of Training in May.

    5.  Practice presenting your idea.

    6.  Attend our pitch contest, bringing confidence and energy for a chance to win money to support you in actually implementing your solution!
